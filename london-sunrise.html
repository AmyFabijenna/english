It is Saturday morning in London. Zoe, Gloria and Martin have a mystery, and you are the one who decides what happens next. Read each chapter, check the words in the boxes, and pick a reply. Every choice leads to a different path. Try them all!

Idioms sound natural, but their meaning is not always logical. Tap a card to flip it and see the meaning, the German translation and an example sentence. You will meet many of these again in the story and in the quiz.

Nine questions, one correct answer each. After you choose, you get immediate feedback with a short explanation. Take your time and think about the grammar and the meaning.

Every time you press the plus sign next to a word in the story, it lands here. Build your personal word bank and review it before you start the next story. Read the words out loud, twice, and try to use each one in a sentence.

Writing is where the language really sticks. Choose one task, write it in your notebook or in the box below, and read it aloud. Keep it short: four to six sentences is perfect.

Task A: A day at Camden Market

Write four sentences about what you would buy, eat or see at the market. Use I would like to… and After that, we…

Task B: A short dialogue

Write four lines between you and Zoe. Use at least two idioms from the Idiom Treasure, for example break the ice or over the moon.

Task C: Word bank challenge

Pick three words from your word bank and write one sentence for each. Try to use one past-tense verb and one comparative (for example bigger or more interesting).

Homework tip: write your text by hand in your notebook first, then check the spelling and the tenses with the word bank.
